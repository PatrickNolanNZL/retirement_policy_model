# Prepared model data files

This folder is for compact prepared model data files only.

Do not place raw, restricted, Treasury, or other source datasets here. Source material belongs outside the released model repository, with prepared extracts generated by documented scripts.

The Treasury LTFM source is the He Tirohanga Mokopuna 2025 workbook, published in September 2025 and filed in the workstream source-data area as `ltfm-htm-sep25.xlsx`.

The app anchors 2026 to the published 1 April 2026 NZS couple net rate. Its base-year net-AOTWE benchmark is calculated from the December 2025 QES gross AOTWE measure, standard `M` tax and the 2026/27 ACC earners' levy, following MSD's published rate-setting method. The benchmark then grows with the LTFM wage path and the statutory CPI corridor projects the current-formula path. The model does not project future tax or ACC settings.

The filed LTFM projection path currently runs from 2026 to 2065 and defines the fiscal modelling horizon.

The Person impacts engine extends the LTFM CPI and wage-growth path beyond 2065 using the terminal LTFM rates. This extension is used only for individual projections where younger current-age cohorts retire after 2065; it does not alter the fiscal tab horizon.

The fiscal income-test histogram is reconstructed from public-source IRD taxable-income bands, MSD NZS/VP payment-rate composition, and Stats NZ living-arrangement age gradients. It uses taxable-income residuals after subtracting category-specific NZS anchors, with negative residuals clipped to zero.

The fiscal benefit-offset calibration uses public-source MSD transfer, MSD stock cross-check, and Work and Income rate data. Its central treatment is fixed in the released model; it has no hidden scenario switches.

The Person impacts tab combines public Stats NZ working-life wage/salary earnings archetypes with separate public-source IRD/MSD non-NZS taxable retirement-income archetypes. The retirement-income archetypes map `low`, `medium`, and `high` to the P25, P50, and P75 of the reconstructed IRD/MSD distribution and are held flat from age 75 onward.
